KEEP THAT! — Always-On Idea Capture

The best take is usually the one nobody was recording. KEEP THAT! keeps a rolling buffer of everything going through it, so when you play something worth keeping and the recorder was not running, you can still get it back.

Press KEEP LAST and pull back the last bars, seconds, or the last musical phrase — trimmed, faded and ready to drag into your project.

This installer can place:

  • VST3 — Live, Cubase, Studio One, REAPER, Bitwig and others
  • Audio Unit — Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage
  • Standalone app — runs on its own, no DAW needed

Universal binary: native on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Requires macOS 11 or later.
