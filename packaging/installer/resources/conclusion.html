Installed

  • VST3 — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/KeepThat.vst3
  • Audio Unit — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/KeepThat.component
  • Standalone — /Applications/KeepThat.app

Logic Pro users: Logic scans Audio Units on launch. If KEEP THAT! does not appear, quit and reopen Logic.

Put it on a track, or on your master bus, and leave it running. It is capturing from the moment it loads — there is nothing to arm.

Captures are written to your chosen destination folder, which you can set by right-clicking any destination button in the plug-in.
