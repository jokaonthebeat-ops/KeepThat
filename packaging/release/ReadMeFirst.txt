KEEP THAT!  —  Always-On Idea Capture
Version 0.9.0  ·  Diamond Loopz
================================================================================

The best take is usually the one nobody was recording. KEEP THAT! is already
listening, so you can get it back.


INSTALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click "Install KeepThat.pkg" and follow the installer. You can choose
which formats to install:

  VST3         /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/KeepThat.vst3
  Audio Unit   /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/KeepThat.component
  Standalone   /Applications/KeepThat.app

The installer is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.


FIRST RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Put KEEP THAT! on a track or your master bus and leave it there. It starts
capturing the moment it loads — there is nothing to arm and no record button.

When you play something worth keeping, press KEEP LAST. Choose how far back to
reach with the buttons underneath: 1, 2, 4 or 8 BARS (these follow your host's
tempo), 15, 30 or 60 SEC, or PHRASE to let it find where the idea started.

The capture appears in RECENT KEEPS. Drag it straight into your project, or
press SAVE WAV.

Logic Pro scans Audio Units at launch. If KEEP THAT! does not appear, quit and
reopen Logic.


GOOD TO KNOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The buffer ROLLS — it never fills up and stops. "AVAILABLE 8:00" means you have
the last eight minutes, not that it has run out.

After each KEEP the buffer restarts, so the clock reads time since your last
idea. Turn that off in SETTINGS if you would rather have one continuous window,
and use SETTINGS > CLEAR BUFFER to restart it by hand.

An eight-minute stereo buffer at 48 kHz holds about 176 MB in RAM. Shorten
BUFFER LENGTH at the bottom left if you would rather have that memory back.

KEEP THAT! makes no network connections. No telemetry, no account, nothing
leaves your machine.


REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
macOS 11 Big Sur or later. Universal — native on Apple Silicon and Intel.
A VST3 host, an AU host (Logic, GarageBand, MainStage), or run it standalone.


UNINSTALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Delete the three paths listed under INSTALL. Your captures and presets live in
~/Library/Application Support/Diamond Loopz/KEEP THAT!/ and are left alone.
