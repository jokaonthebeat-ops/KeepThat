=== SHORT (one line, <=140 chars) ===
KEEP THAT! is always listening. Play something great with the recorder off,
press one button, and pull the last 8 bars straight back.

=== MEDIUM (store card, ~60 words) ===
Always-on idea capture. KEEP THAT! holds a rolling eight minutes of everything
going through it, so the take you didn't record isn't gone. Recover the last
bars, seconds or musical phrase - trimmed, faded, key-labelled and ready to
drag into your project. Nothing to arm, no added latency, and the buffer
restarts each time you keep something. Free. VST3/AU/Standalone, macOS
universal + Windows.

=== LONG (product page) ===
Use Marketing/SALES-PAGE.md.

=== FEATURE BULLETS ===
- Rolling buffer up to 8 minutes - always capturing, nothing to arm
- Recover 1/2/4/8 bars (host tempo), 15/30/60 seconds, or the last PHRASE
- Phrase detection finds where the idea actually started
- Auto trim, silence detect, zero-crossing snap, fades, normalise - applied
  as the capture is made
- Key detection (chroma + Krumhansl-Kessler) and tempo estimation when the
  host reports none
- Buffer restarts after each KEEP, so the clock reads time since your last idea
- 100 recent keeps with thumbnails, rename, favourite - and undo that never
  deletes the file
- Drag straight to your DAW, or 24-bit WAV to folder / sampler / desktop /
  playlist with .m3u
- Zero added latency; audio thread never allocates, locks or hits the disk
- No network, no telemetry, no account
- VST3 + AU + Standalone, macOS 11+ universal; Windows VST3 + Standalone

=== REQUIREMENTS ===
macOS 11 Big Sur or later, Intel or Apple Silicon.
Windows 10 or later, 64-bit.
VST3 host, AU host (Logic/GarageBand), or standalone.
